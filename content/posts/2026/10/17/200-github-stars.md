Today, the [OpenFastTrace repository](https://github.com/itsallcode/openfasttrace) reached 200 stars on GitHub.

We are especially proud of this milestone because requirement tracing is still a narrow niche in software development, even more so for a non-commercial open-source tool. It is encouraging to see that OFT resonates with people who care about keeping requirements, designs, code and tests connected.

Thank you to all our users and contributors who have gone with us through more than ten years of OFT. You are the best!
